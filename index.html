PixelClip

Remove backgrounds, upscale, erase objects, restore old photos and more. Runs on your PC. Your photos never leave it.

Download for Windows

Free · about 2.6 GB · Windows 10 or 11 · works offline · Version 1.0

How to start

  1. Download the file and wait for it to finish.
  2. Right-click PixelClip.zip, choose Extract All, then Extract.
  3. Open the PixelClip folder and double-click PixelClip.exe.

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"?

This is normal for new apps. Click More info, then Run anyway.

What you can do

  • Remove backgrounds
  • Enhance and sharpen
  • Upscale 2x or 4x
  • Erase objects
  • Restore old photos
  • Portrait retouch
  • Blur background
  • Hide faces
  • Vectorize logos

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • About 6 GB of free disk space once unzipped
  • 8 GB of RAM recommended

FAQ

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download it. After that it works offline.

Why is the first launch slower?

PixelClip prepares its AI tools the first time it opens. Later launches are faster.
